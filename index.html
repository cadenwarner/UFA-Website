Analyst Program

The Analyst Program is designed for underclassmen and new members with no prior finance experience. In addition to the General Member meeting, analysts meet weekly for dedicated training sessions to build the skills needed to succeed in UFA and beyond.

Each week, our Directors of Investments and Consulting lead sessions covering accounting, valuation, financial modeling, and market analysis: everything you need to hit the ground running in UFA and in recruiting.

Analyst Competitions

Every analyst competes in the fall and the spring.

Fall Semester

Stock Pitch Competition

Analyst teams select any publicly traded stock on a U.S. exchange, build their investment thesis, and defend it in front of a panel. Top teams advance to a final round.

Format: Round 1 open to all analyst teams. Top 6 advance to finals.
Universe: Any stock listed on a U.S. exchange. Team selects the company.
Judges: UFA upperclassmen and finance alumni.
Prize pool: $1,000 split across 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.
Spring Semester

Case Competition

Analyst teams are assigned a real corporate case and given approximately one week to develop and present their recommendation.

Format: Round 1 open to all analyst teams. Top 6 advance to finals.
Client: A real corporation. Case details released one week before presentations.
Judges: Active industry professionals from the finance and business community.
Prize pool: $1,000 split across 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.
Experience required · application + interview

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The University Finance Association ("UFA," "we," "us") is a student organization at The University of Texas at Austin. This policy explains what we collect through texasufa.org, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

If you just browse the site

We use Google Analytics, which collects standard usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, referring site, and device and browser type. We use this only to understand which pages people find useful. We do not try to identify individual visitors from it.

If you use the contact form

We collect the name, email address, and message you submit. Messages are delivered to our board inbox through EmailJS, a third-party email service. We use this only to reply to you.

If you have a member portal account

The portal stores your name, UT EID, email address, expected graduation year, your role in UFA, and your account password. It also stores a record of your event attendance — which meetings and events you checked into, on what dates, and the participation points you earned. Your name and point total are visible to other signed-in members on the portal leaderboard.

Where your information is stored

Member portal data is stored with Supabase, a third-party database provider. The site itself is served through Cloudflare. Both providers process data on our behalf and are subject to their own security and privacy practices.

Who can see it

Member information is accessible only to the UFA executive board. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with sponsors, recruiters, employers, or any other outside party. We may disclose information if required to by law or by university policy.

How long we keep it

We retain member records for as long as you are a member and for a reasonable period afterward so the board can maintain organizational history. You may ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your choices

You can view and update your name, email, and graduation year yourself on the Profile tab of the member portal. You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing us. Most browsers also let you block analytics cookies, and doing so will not affect your ability to use the site.

Students under 18

This site is intended for university students. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can go to financeassociationtx@gmail.com.
